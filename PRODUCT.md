# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), chosen by the user over WordPress because ISTA staff will not edit the site; a developer makes all content changes. Free static hosting (Netlify / Cloudflare Pages) is the intended target. Enquiry form via a free form service (to be decided).

## Users

Three audiences of equal weight on the homepage (user decision):

- **Students and their parents** in Hyderabad / Telangana planning to study abroad (Australia, UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand, Europe). Parents are often the ones deciding and paying.
- **Professionals and families** seeking permanent residency in Australia or Canada.
- **People needing a single service**: certificate attestation, a visit visa, or IELTS / PTE coaching.

Most arrive on a phone, often from a WhatsApp share or a local Google search.

## Product Purpose

ISTA Consultancy Services is an overseas education and immigration consultancy. The website exists to make a first-time visitor trust ISTA enough to call, WhatsApp, or send an enquiry for a free counselling session. Success = enquiries and calls.

## Positioning

A local, in-person Hyderabad office (Tolichowki) operating since 2013 that handles the whole journey under one roof: choosing a country and course, admissions, visa, test preparation (IELTS / PTE), documents (attestation), and PR immigration for Australia and Canada.

## Operating Context

- Visitors usually book an in-person or phone counselling session before committing.
- Decisions involve families, money, and long timelines (intakes, visa processing).
- Contact is by phone, WhatsApp, email, or walking into the office.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services (confirmed from the 2018 site):

- Study abroad counselling and admissions: Australia, UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand, Europe
- Immigration: Australia and Canada PR
- Certificate attestation
- Visit visa assistance
- IELTS coaching, PTE training

"Web Solutions" from the old site is **excluded** (user decision).

Site pages (18 on the old site, 17 after merging the two attestation URLs): Home, About, Services, six study destinations, Australia Immigration, Canada Immigration, Certificate Attestation, Visit Visa Assistance, IELTS, PTE, Enquiry, Contact. Only the homepage is in scope now; other pages come later. Old `.html` URLs should redirect to new routes at launch.

- Logo: `assets/logo.png` (blue sphere + yellow swoosh forming an "i", blue "ISTA" wordmark, tagline). Must be used as is.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: specifics and real contact details beat slogans.
2. Every service is one clear path to a conversation (call, WhatsApp, enquiry).
3. Never promise outcomes ISTA cannot control (visas, admissions).
4. Works fast and reads well on a mid-range phone on mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Many visitors read English as a second language: plain words, short sentences. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
